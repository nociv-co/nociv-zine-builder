Printing & Paper Selection

📄 Paper Weight Recommendations:

  • Standard 20lb / 75gsm Copy Paper: Ideal for 8-page micro zines so folds remain crisp without excessive bulk.
  • 28lb / 105gsm Text Paper: Best for 16-page pocket booklets & saddle-stitched zines for rich opacity and durability.

⚠️ Printer Scaling Guardrail:

Always set printer scaling to 100% (Actual Size). Do NOT select "Fit to Printable Area" as this distorts the center fold alignment.

2

8-Page Micro Zine Folding Sequence (Hotdog & Slit)

Horizontal Fold
A. Hotdog Fold

Fold the printed sheet in half lengthwise (hotdog style), then open it back flat.

Vertical Folds
B. Hamburger Grid

Fold in half widthwise, then fold outer edges in to create an 8-panel grid.

✂️ Slit Center
C. Center Slit Cut

Fold widthwise and cut along center horizontal line between middle 2 panels ONLY.

✦ Starburst
D. Push & Collapse

Push ends together into a 4-point star shape, then wrap front cover over into booklet.

Binding Options for Medium & Large Booklets

METHOD 1: SADDLE STAPLING HACK 📌

Don't have a long-arm booklet stapler? Use a standard desk stapler + pink pencil eraser!

  1. Open your standard stapler flat 180°.
  2. Place an eraser under the center spine line of your folded booklet.
  3. Press staple through spine into the eraser.
  4. Pull eraser off and manually fold staple prongs flat with your fingernail or coin!
METHOD 2: 3-HOLE PAMPHLET SEWING 🪡

Classic tactile bookbinding using thread, wax cord, or embroidery floss.

  1. Poke 3 holes along center crease (Top, Middle, Bottom).
  2. Start needle through Middle hole from inside to outside.
  3. Go into Top hole, down past middle to Bottom hole.
  4. Re-enter through Middle hole and tie knot around long center thread loop!
